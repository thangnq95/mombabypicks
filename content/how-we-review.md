## How We Review Baby Products

Mom Baby Picks is built to help new parents make calmer buying decisions. Baby gear can be confusing, expensive, and full of marketing claims, so our reviews focus on practical details that matter in daily life.

We do not recommend a product just because it is popular. Before a product appears in a guide, we compare its features, safety notes, parent feedback, price range, and how it fits a specific use case.

## What We Look For

### Safety and Everyday Use

For baby products, safety comes first. We look for details such as BPA-free materials, closed-system pump design, auto-shutoff features, safe sleep guidance, cleaning requirements, and age or weight limits where relevant.

### Real Parent Feedback

We read patterns across customer reviews instead of focusing on one dramatic review. If parents repeatedly mention leaking, poor battery life, difficult cleaning, or confusing controls, we factor that into the recommendation.

### Value for the Money

The most expensive product is not always the best choice. We try to explain who should choose a budget option, who should upgrade, and when a premium feature is actually useful.

### Fit for a Specific Parent

A wearable pump may be best for a working mom, while a hospital-grade electric pump may be better for exclusive pumping. A bottle warmer that works for formula may not be the best choice for carefully warming breast milk.
