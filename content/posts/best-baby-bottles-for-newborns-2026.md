## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many baby bottles do I actually need?**
Start with 4-6. You'll wash them constantly in the early weeks, so having a small rotation is enough. If you're pumping and storing milk, you may want a few more.

**At what age can I switch to a faster-flow nipple?**
Most brands recommend moving to a medium flow around 3 months, but watch your baby's cues. If they're gulping, gagging, or spitting up after feeds, the flow is too fast. If they're working really hard and getting frustrated, it may be too slow.

**Do I need to sterilize bottles every time?**
In the newborn stage, yes — especially if your baby was premature or has a weakened immune system. For healthy babies, sterilizing once a day and washing thoroughly between feeds is generally fine. Check with your pediatrician.

**My baby refuses every bottle I try. What do I do?**
This is more common than people talk about. Try warming the nipple tip under warm water before offering it, having someone other than the primary breastfeeding parent give the bottle, and experimenting with timing (not starving, not completely full). Some babies need two or three weeks to accept a bottle consistently.

**Can I mix brands — like use a Dr. Brown's bottle with an Avent nipple?**
Most brands don't cross-compatible, and mixing can affect the venting system and cause leaks. Stick to matched sets, especially for anti-colic systems where the vent relies on the whole bottle working together.
