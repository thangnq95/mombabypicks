Bottle Refusal in Breastfed Babies: What to Try First

Bottle refusal can happen even when breastfeeding is going well. The issue is often not the bottle itself but the timing, temperature, nipple shape, or who is offering it. Things to try Offer the bottle when baby is calm, not starving Let someone else offer it Try a different nipple shape Warm the nipple slightly before feeding Use paced bottle feeding When to keep it simple If one bottle style consistently fails, switch to a different shape rather than repeating the same struggle.
